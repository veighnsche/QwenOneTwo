  semantic information, separability or recoverability.
- Noul class-logit ranges, unchanged-threshold correctness, AUC and between/within

  claim; family/pair correlation remains visible.
- Score and Noul actual behavior plus paired raw changes, separately from exact
  target fidelity/status. Do not widen Score intervals or change the Noul threshold.

Acceptance: complete planned capture, unchanged frozen identities and cached
features, exact replay, valid transport/accounting, reproducible statistics and
honest interpretation. A negative or inconclusive information result is a valid
completed diagnostic, not a semantic qualification. No arbitrary wall-clock cap
or automatic training/search follows. Preserve partial captures on failure.

--output data/<fresh-run> --execute-gpu`, with the primary UUID explicitly selected.
