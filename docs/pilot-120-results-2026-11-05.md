## Review and repairs

Six single-call authoring batches generated 16 cases per domain. Six fresh-session
reviews each examined 20 cases: four existing plus 16 new. Review prompts contained
the task, supplied facts/rules, request semantics and operation; proposed status,
payload, Reason and continuation were withheld. Each reviewer independently
derived outcomes and supplied concrete semantic/outcome judgments. Per-record
artifacts bind the actual shared prompt/response bytes and final annotation hash.

Codex checked the results and corrected concrete findings: unknown candidates
mislabelled false, eligibility confused with ranking, unsupported candidate
attributes, missing rubric/bank fields, ambiguous time/formula wording and two
near-duplicate contexts. One 21-case blind review checked disputed/revised cases.
The final duplicate gate caught another repeated slide-deck prompt; it was
replaced with a geometric screen-coverage task and received one fresh review.
No automatic regeneration or repeated review loop ran. JSON punctuation/null-key
repairs were local and explicitly recorded. The raw generated bytes were kept.

Final annotations adopt the separately derived review outcomes/Reason/
continuations after these content checks. Initial proposals and changes remain
in the audit trail. All24 original input/semantic objects were preserved in the
imported cases; their original 125 run files are byte-for-byte unchanged.
