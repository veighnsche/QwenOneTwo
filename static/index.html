UNDER THE HOOD

A small head. Real weights.

Qev’s decision adapter is enabled for the initial scalar thinking budget, then disabled for normal generation. A trained internal gate can request Noul, Choice or Score during thinking and answering. A learned hidden vector carries the request and a learned feedback vector returns its result. No separate decision request is sent by the browser.

Experimental training

The learned gate, query and result feedback are trained on a small synthetic curriculum. The budget uses scalar regression against illustrative effort preferences, not measured optimal budgets. Broad decision quality and latent invocation generalization are not established. The adapter modes share a hybrid cache; compatibility is experimental.

Training provenance
How the budget is enforced

Every run prefills the prompt once. Zero closes the open thinking section through that same cache without sampling reasoning. Positive budgets cap sampled thinking tokens. At the cap, the closing thinking delimiter is fed through the same hybrid cache before the final answer. Natural early closure is preserved.

Unused output rows never enter generated text. Prompt tokens are excluded; sampled and injected control tokens are counted separately below each response. A budget can interrupt a thought; an exhausted answer allowance can truncate an answer.
